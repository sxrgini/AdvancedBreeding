### Advanced Breeding (Fabric/NeoForge)

Fabric \& NeoForge 26.2+ - Version 1.0.0



##### **How does this mod work?**

* This mod expands upon Breeding by adding stats and traits to all Breedable mobs based on the survival game Icarus.



* All mobs now have Male and Female counterparts and Females can only be bred with Males to produce children.



* You can now Crouch and Right Click mobs to open up the Advanced Breeding GUI. This GUI shows you Genotype, Phenotype (if it applies), Parents (if it’s a baby you’ve bred), and Bloodline.



* There is a new multi block structure. All you do is place 4 Hay Bales then Crouch and Right Click to turn it into a Hay Bed.



* Mobs that are breed-able have had their spawning reworked. You are not able to tame Adult Wild mobs you instead take their children and tame them. Babies have a 50% chance to spawn with an Adult mob in the Wild.



* To tame a baby mob you must do the following:

&#x20;  —Kill the Parent of the baby

&#x20;  —Attach to a lead (Only to begin taming)

&#x20;  —Place a bed within 10 Blocks

&#x20;  —Feed the mob

&#x20;  —Place within a fenced area



* Genotypes

&#x20;  —*Genotypes* are the mob’s stats. Any stat above Level 3 is a Positive Stat, Any stat that is below Level 3 becomes a Negative Stat. When breeding there is a 50% chance to inherit Moms Stats and 50% to inherit Dads stats, but there is also a 25% chance to Mutate and gains better stats than both Parents.

&#x20;  —**Vigor** - Max Health +5%

&#x20;  —**Fitness** - Jump Strength +5%

&#x20;  —**Physique** - Strength Aura +8%

&#x20;  —**Reflex** - Movement Speed +4%

&#x20;  —**Toughness** - Physical Damage Taken -4%

&#x20;  —**Adaptation** - Fire and Freeze Damage Taken -6%

&#x20;  —**Instinct** - Baby Growth, Egg Laying, and Wool Growth Speed +8%



* Phenotypes

&#x20;  —*Phenotype* shows the variant of the animal (Warm, Temperate, Colors, etc.)
—Phenotypes can also *Mutate* resulting in an baby that is a different color from both Parents. *90% Chance* to take on a Parents color, *10% Chance* to Mutate.



* Bloodlines

&#x20;  —*Bloodlines* are special traits that grant new stats as mobs level up.

&#x20;  -**Wild** - 40.82% - +5% Food Consumption, +1% chance to cause Poison on Hit with Attacks (Every 5 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Brave** - 6.80% - -10% Jump Height, +1% Melee Damage (Every 2 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Careful** - 6.80% - -10% Movement Speed, +0.05% Melee Damage (Every 2 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Timid** - 6.80% - -10% Melee Damage, +1% Movement Speed (Every 1 Level)

&#x20;  -**Bold** - 6.80% - -10 Physical Resistance, +0.5% Jump Height (Every 2 Levels), -1% Saturation Rate (Every 2 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Hardy** - 6.80% - +10% Food Consumption, +1% Maximum Health (Every 2 Levels), -1% Saturation Rate (Every 2 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Stout** - 6.80% - +10% Perceived Threat, +1% Physical Resistance (Every 2 Levels)
   -**Ambitious** - 6.80% - +50% Experience Gained

&#x20;  -**Resolute** - 3.40% - -10% Maximum Health, -1% Saturation Rate (Every 2 Levels), +1% Fertilization Recovery (Every 1 Level)

&#x20;  -**Unstable** - 3.40% - -50% Fertilization Recovery Speed, +1% Genotype Mutation Chance (Every 2 Levels), +1% Phenotype Mutation Chance (Every 2 Levels)

&#x20;  -**Savage** - 3.40% - -50% Health Regeneration, 1% chance to Leech Health on Hit with Attacks (Every 1 Level)

&#x20;  -**Alpha** - 1.36% - +20% Size Increase, +1% Melee Damage (Every 1 Level), +1% Maximum Health (Every 1 Level)





##### **Traits**



* Traits are upgrades you can give to your tamed mobs. Most mobs have the same Base Traits but then also have other Traits that only apply to that specific mob.
